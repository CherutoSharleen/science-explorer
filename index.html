Offline-ready as a complete folder. Download and unzip the site, then open index.html. Keep its CSS, JavaScript and other files together: animations, explanations, practice and revision PDFs need no internet. Simply visiting the hosted site does not install an offline app. Progress stays separately for each topic in the current browser, not in a school account; local-file storage can vary between browsers.

Videos are optional. The two earlier topics include click-to-load, no-autoplay YouTube embeds. The five new topics use YouTube search links, not pre-approved individual videos. External sites need internet and may show adverts, recommendations or tracking. A teacher should preview results for accuracy, safety and age suitability.

Learn from the source. Respect the source.

These are original teaching resources informed by the KICD Grade 8 Integrated Science curriculum design, revised 2024. The curriculum PDF is not hosted or included. The seven downloadable revision guides are newly written lesson notes and question banks, not copies of the source.

Independent educational prototype, not KICD-endorsed. Teacher review required before classroom use. These introductions do not replace supervised practical work, formal assessment or personal medical advice. Fire lessons are virtual only; health lessons request no personal dates, symptoms or sexual history.

Put this static site on GitHub Pages

Unzip the deployment bundle and upload its contents to your repository, with index.html at the root rather than inside another folder. In repository Settings > Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, select your branch and the root folder, then save. Use GitHub's published Pages URL once deployment finishes. No build command, server or API key is needed. The free Pages option is available for public repositories.

Keep all generated science-*.css and science-*.js files from the same bundle as the HTML. Their versioned filenames allow browsers to reuse shared files between topics while picking up updates.

No student login, analytics, backend or paid AI service. Source access remains subject to the owner's permissions. External hosting and video providers have their own privacy policies.
